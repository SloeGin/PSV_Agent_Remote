AGENT//REMOTE · REPLY DRAWER

Mockups of the reply drawer, at 75% of the Vita's 960×544. Highlights show where the cursor is.

Drawer rules

□ opens the drawer when the session has no decision. □ again (or ○) closes it: fully when there's no draft, to a one-line peek when there is a draft or a queued reply. ✕ presses the highlighted button; nothing is highlighted when it opens.

Decisions win

While a decision is pending, □ belongs to the decision sheet and the reply peek is hidden. A decision that arrives while the keyboard is open takes over as soon as the keyboard closes; the draft comes back as a peek once the decision is resolved.
